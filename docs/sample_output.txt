Bazar.com - Sample Output
=========================

The three services were started with "docker compose up --build -d"
using fresh databases (5 copies of each book). The client ran on the
host against the frontend at http://localhost:5000.

Scenario:
  1. search both topics
  2. info for book 2 and for a book that does not exist (99)
  3. buy book 2 five times (all 5 copies)
  4. buy book 2 once more (out of stock)
  5. info for book 2 again (stock is 0)
  6. buy a book that does not exist (99)
